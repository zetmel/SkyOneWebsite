Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. Who we are

Sky One Marketing (Pvt) Ltd ("Sky One", "we", "us") operates this website to present our product catalogue and to receive quotation and availability requests. We are a supplier of aluminium profiles, glass hardware, ceiling systems, cladding sheets, kitchen sinks and architectural accessories in Sri Lanka.

2. Information we collect

When you use the contact form on this website, we may collect your name, company, phone number, email address, customer type, the products you are interested in, and your message. This information is collected only when you choose to provide it.

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, confirm product availability and specifications, and provide customer support. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and browser storage

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
