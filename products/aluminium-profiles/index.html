About Aluminium Profiles

Sky One Marketing supplies a range of aluminium extrusion profiles used by fabricators, contractors and workshops across Sri Lanka. Profiles are available for doors, windows, sliding systems, partitions, shopfronts, curtain-wall elements and general structural and decorative fabrication. For details on available sections, lengths, finishes and current stock, contact the Sky One team directly.
