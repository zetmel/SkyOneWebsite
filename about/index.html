About Sky One Marketing

Sky One Marketing (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lankan supplier of aluminium profiles, glass hardware and architectural materials — serving fabricators, contractors, builders, architects, designers and homeowners.

What we supply

Our catalogue is organised for the way construction projects actually work. Fabricators can identify glass fittings by model number — such as the VVP patch fittings FT10, FT20, FT30, FT40, FT50 and FT60. Contractors can find ceiling-grid components, cladding sheets, rivets and sealants. Dealers and homeowners can browse kitchen sinks and door and window hardware.

We are a quotation-based supplier: you request a price, we confirm availability, specifications and finishes, and you order with confidence. New product ranges are added as the business grows.

How we work

  • Clear ranges

  • Straightforward quoting

    Request a quotation by phone, email or the online form.

  • Confirmed details
